# Public Economics and Development (Spring 2026, NTHU)


This is the repository containing the slides (pdf), codes to compile these slides (tex), and other course materials used for the Publinc Economics and Development class in Spring 2026 semester at Taipei School of Economics, National Tsing Hua University. 

This class covers topics in public economics, focusing on the challenges governments in middle- and low-income countries face. The course is organized around four major topics

- Stylized facts and classical theories on optimal taxation
- Fiscal capacity in developing countries
- Anti-poverty programs and pubic service provision
- Organizational capacity of the state

Specifically, the course covers the foundations of the optimal taxation theories and why it may not apply to developing countries, differences in tax collection in developing countries, impact of informal economies on taxation, debates on cash vs. in-kind and conditional vs. unconditional, principal-agent problems in public organizations, recruitment of individuals to the public sector, and retention of existing public sector workers. 

The course is aimed at post-graduate students and well-prepared undergraduates in economics and political science. It assumes familiarity with statistical inference and undergraduate level micro/macroeconomics. Each week is built around  discussion of 5-7 seminal empirical papers that cover both the key theoretical frameworks and the causal evidence based on rigorous empirical studies. 

The materials are shared for reference and teaching purposes. You are welcome to adapt them with attribution. Please note that the reading list and assigned papers remain the intellectual property of their respective authors and publishers.
